SYNTHETIC TOY DATA — NOT BIOLOGICAL OBSERVATIONS

The six CSVs in raw/ are generated by generate_toy_data.py with seed 42.
They contain 80 artificial cell lines, eight artificial cancer-type labels,
40 artificial patient groups, and 20 artificial outcome genes.

There are 24 expression, 16 copy-number, and 12 binary mutation inputs.
The outcome construction uses simulated expression, copy number, mutation,
lineage and noise. Five synthetic targets are labelled common essential and
five are low-variation comparison targets. None is an actual human gene.

Run python run_toy.py from the package root. It exercises the actual parsers,
alignment, grouped evaluation, feature selection, ridge and scoring code.
Outputs go to work/toy/ and are separate from the archived real-data results.
